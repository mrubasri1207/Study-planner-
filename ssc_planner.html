💪 Motivation & Consistency Tips

⏰ Time Management
  • • Set phone alarms 10 min before each session
  • • Prepare study area night before (desk setup)
  • • Start with a 2-min warm-up (read notes/review)
  • • College ends when you close books - no half-study
🎯 Consistency Hacks
  • • Mark ✅ today - feel the instant win
  • • Don't skip a day - 2 skips = motivation death
  • • If missed AM, do 2-hour evening session
  • • Celebrate weekly: weekend cheat meal after 7/7 days
🧠 Learning Hack
  • • Watch video on 1.25x speed (saves 10% time)
  • • Pause & write 3 key points after each video
  • • Practice questions = active recall (most important)
  • • One mistake = revisit that concept ONLY
⚡ Staying Energized
  • • 5 AM: Drink water, light breakfast (banana/milk)
  • • Mid-session (7 PM): 2-min walk break
  • • No phone during study (WhatsApp = dopamine trap)
  • • Track progress - seeing 70%+ gives energy!

🚀 Consistency beats perfection. One day at a time. You've got this!
